Short, premium, commerce-oriented. Use clear rental and purchase wording. Avoid long introductions.

## Toast Patterns

- `请先选择骑行人`
- `网络异常，请稍后重试`
- `该骑行人已添加`
- `删除失败，请稍后重试`

## Avoid

- Long marketing paragraphs.
- Over-explaining tab or carousel behavior.
- Dark hardware-control words like `滑动开锁`, `蓝牙无感开锁`, `玩灯工坊`.
